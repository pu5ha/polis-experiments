# Seed statements — The Issuance Curve

Live conversation: [pol.is/9xjdymbbhw](https://pol.is/9xjdymbbhw)

These are the statements originally seeded into the conversation, distilled from the [EIP-8363 "Tapered Issuance & Burn" thread](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8363-tapered-issuance-burn/29263) on Ethereum Magicians. They span both sides of the debate — whether to taper issuance, how to protect solo stakers, how urgent the problem is, and how much is still genuinely uncertain — without taking a position themselves.

**Note:** some of these have since been removed directly on pol.is (the live set is down to 18 seed statements as of the last edit). This file reflects the original seed list for provenance — pol.is/9xjdymbbhw is the source of truth for what's actually live.

1. Ethereum already has enough staked ETH to make a direct economic attack prohibitively expensive.
2. Ethereum should continue increasing the amount of ETH securing the network, even if attacks are already very expensive.
3. More staked ETH does not always make Ethereum more secure.
4. Ethereum should optimize for the composition of stake, not just the amount of stake.
5. Ethereum currently issues more ETH for staking than is necessary.
6. Ethereum's staking rewards should eventually fall toward zero.
7. Ethereum should always maintain a non-zero minimum staking yield.
8. Unlimited growth in the amount of staked ETH is a problem Ethereum should solve.
9. Ethereum should have a natural equilibrium where staking eventually stops growing.
10. It's acceptable if nearly all ETH eventually becomes staked.
11. The current issuance curve does not have a clear long-term stopping point.
12. Ethereum should prioritize policies that improve solo staking.
13. Lower staking rewards are more likely to hurt solo stakers than large operators.
14. Delegated staking naturally becomes more attractive as staking grows.
15. Liquid staking protocols becoming more important is a long-term risk for Ethereum.
16. Centralized exchanges becoming larger staking providers is a bigger risk than liquid staking protocols.
17. Ethereum should avoid becoming dependent on a small number of staking intermediaries.
18. Native staking yield is an important reason institutions buy ETH.
19. Lower ETH issuance could make ETH more attractive even if staking yields decline.
20. Ethereum should prioritize sound monetary policy over maintaining a high staking yield.
21. Ethereum should move cautiously when changing issuance policy.
22. The risks of leaving the issuance curve unchanged are underestimated.
23. The community needs more evidence before changing Ethereum's issuance curve.
24. No one knows what the ideal staking ratio for Ethereum actually is.
25. The tradeoffs around Ethereum's issuance curve are still too uncertain to confidently choose one direction.
